STAT 101: Introductory Statistics (SYNTHETIC SYLLABUS written for the Squad demo; not an OpenStax document)

Instructor: Dr. Rivera. Teaching assistants hold office hours on Thursdays.

Course materials
The course follows excerpts of OpenStax Introductory Statistics 2e (chapters 2, 3, 6 and 12).

Grading
Weekly homework sets are graded for correctness and make up 30% of the final grade. There are two midterms and a final exam.

Homework policy
Homework sets are graded work. You may discuss concepts with classmates, but you must write up your own solutions.
Using an AI assistant to produce the final answer to a graded homework problem is not allowed. Asking an AI for hints, explanations of the course readings, or practice problems is allowed.
Ungraded practice problems and textbook exercises may be worked on with any help you like.

Academic integrity
Submitting work that is not your own, including answers copied from an AI assistant, is a violation of the academic integrity policy.
