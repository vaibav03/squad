## What does not / known problems
- The camera does not follow the replay action: in the late frames (t=100s track2, and most of track3) the view sits on an empty stretch of road with only the player and a bubble, so a viewer cannot see what the agents are doing. No auto-pan to the active agents.
- Replay badge (my glue) overlaps the top-right HUD "?" button.
- First frame (t=0) is a blank dark panel while Phaser loads (about 1-2 s), plus a tiny stage letterbox in the shell before ready.
- No overlays: call panel, quiz card, confirm sheet are not opened by village overlay events (only logged bottom-left).
- Personas page shows "Could not load personas" in the capture because the capture used a fake token (401); sign in through the stub or dev-login to test.
- Console: only WebGL "GPU stall due to ReadPixels" performance warnings (software GL in headless). 401s on /personas as above. No page errors.
- Real keys, iMessage, audio and media services were not exercised; the brain/mesh/media/spine services are not wired into the hub or bot.
