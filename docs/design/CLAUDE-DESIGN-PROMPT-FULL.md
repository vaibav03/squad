I: Design the complete Squad product, phone (390px) and desktop (1280px), as one coherent system. A. THE CAMPUS (walkable, top-down, four directions; arrow keys/WASD, tap-to-walk with pathfinding, a virtual joystick option on phone). The student walks their own character. Rooms and what each is for (each room has a door sign, a "what this room does" panel on entry, and shows an outcome card at the end):
1. Commons (living-room theme): the hub with a big shared table, plants, a fireplace, a notice board and a reception desk. The notice board is the Glass Box ledger: what each agent shared versus what it kept private (withheld = a static black bar).
2. Chapter classrooms (classroom and library theme), one per chapter off a corridor: desks, chalkboard, a lamp lit when its expert is awake, and a mastery glow on the door frame (dim to bright). Inside: the chapter expert answers questions with cited sources ("[ch6.pdf p.4]"); a doubt, a quiz round, a Feynman check and Step Diff (two students' photographed work, shows only where it first splits, never a verdict) all start here.
3. Council Room (conference hall theme, round table): "Ask the council": three members with different stances answer independently (thought bubbles), rank each other anonymously, then a chairman gives the verdict, keeps the dissenting view in a lone red-lined bubble, and says what to do next. Disagreement flickers a lamp ("uncertain, ask your TA"). The student types the question in the room; the transcript shows every member's actual words; a verdict card ends it with a copy button.
4. Course-Planning Round Table: chill, grind and advisor agents plus a chairman build a next-semester plan from the course catalogue and the student's completed courses (prerequisites are checked by code, not argued). Outcome card: course list, credit total, workload in words (never grades), the dissent in one line, and a "why" per agent.
5. Debate Hall (conference hall): two experts at lecterns argue opposite answers; the group votes with coloured cards; the chairman explains.
6. Feynman Corner (living-room couch): a confused freshman asks naive "why"s while the student explains; feedback only, no score.
7. Panic Line (hospital theme, night-tinted): a 2am calm-down room that picks the three topics worth the most before the exam.
8. Oral Exam Room: a strict examiner and one chair; a voice call. 9. Rubric Room: pre-grades work against the real rubric, feedback only. 10. Exam Oracle (the library's reading room): predicts exam questions from past exams. 11. Devil's TA (messy back office, basement theme): hides one mistake in a chalkboard explanation for the student to catch. 12. Homework-help desk: gives a hint, a citation and the syllabus policy, never the answer to graded work. 13. Podcast studio: a short two-host audio recap of a chapter, personal or squad cut. 14. Cafeteria (kitchen theme): the Lock-In Lobby, booking a 50-minute co-study session; calendar invites. 15. Reception: setup of phone, course PDFs and .ics calendar.
Locked or dimmed rooms show a padlock sprite until relevant.

B. THE AGENT MESH (Track-2 story). Each neighbour student's private room sits along a side corridor behind a locked door. Agents negotiate by walking out to the Commons table carrying envelopes; speech bubbles show only shared fields; a conflict shows a red "!"; an agent waiting for its student's yes stands at its own door with a pulsing ring; when every student confirms, a trip cutscene plays once and is skippable (the camera pans to the lobby windows, a campus shuttle pulls up and drives off, the Trip door opens). Also design: the private study-slot negotiation (free times matched without anyone seeing calendars), the trip planner page (options, my private limits visible only to me, secret-vote progress with no names, confirmations, equal cost split), and the "request denied: private" moment when a friend's agent asks for something it may not have.

Also a small chat dock inside the campus so the student can ask without leaving the world. Include iMessage mockups of the same moments (a cited doubt, a quiz round, a private "am I behind?" DM, a confirm question with numbered options, a podcast audio bubble).

MOTION (Emil Kowalski's rules; one plan for canvas and DOM): gate every animation by frequency (100+/day, tens/day, occasional, rare) and one purpose (feedback, spatial consistency, state indication, preventing a jarring change, explanation, delight). Walking, passing a door, Esc and opening the minimap get no animation beyond the walk cycle; delight only for the trip shuttle and first arrival. Tokens, defined once and shared: --ease-out cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1) (enter/exit), --ease-in-out cubic-bezier(0.77, 0, 0.175, 1) (moving), --ease-drawer cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1) (panels); one rare spring { duration: 0.5, bounce: 0.2 } for the Trip door only; never ease-in, never transition: all, never scale(0); DOM animates only transform and opacity. Rows to deliver in the motion table: player walk (4 directions, 12 fps, constant 36 px/s, instant start, 80ms ease-out stop, no camera lag), NPC pathfinding walks (same speed, 60ms stagger when several leave), ambient life (lamp flicker, plant sway, clock: 2 frames), door open/close (frame swap only, locked doors never animate), door pulse ring while waiting for a yes (1.6s ease-out loop; check draws by mask and the lock opens in 200ms), envelope carry (180ms pop, 320ms glide onto the table), speech and thought bubbles (160ms in, 120ms out, hold 1.2s per 20 characters), conflict "!" (160ms, no shake), council sequence (80ms bubble stagger, 320ms slide to rank slots, 240ms chairman, dissent lamp never above 3 Hz), camera crossfade between rooms (200ms) and skippable HUD jump pan (400ms), room enter/leave fade (200ms; Esc is instant), overlay panels (@starting-style, translateY 8px plus opacity, 200-280ms ease-drawer), speaking agent in calls (2-frame mouth swap at 8 fps, 2px bob, glow follows audio), HUD chip and minimap changes (150-200ms), buttons (press 0.97, hover only in (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)), the shuttle cutscene (pan 400ms, 2.4s slide, spring on the Trip door, plays once, any key skips), Replay/Live badge (150ms). Words never move for style (captions, ledger, quiz text, bubbles appear whole). Every row has a reduced-motion/Calm variant (fewer and gentler, not zero). Pause everything when the tab is hidden; nothing flashes more than 3 times a second.
