# Brief: village (spec 09), branch ws/village  [UI workstream: top-notch; the star of the product]

**Mission:** packages/village: the Phaser 3.90 walkable side-view pixel night world that shows real events happening. This is the hero; it must look like a real indie game.

**Build:** boot and scene manager; hand-placed layout; 5 to 6 parallax layers with day/night ramps; the student's character with constant-speed walking, keyboard and tap-to-walk, camera follow and Left/Mid/Right/Zoom; chapter houses with signboards, lit windows and mastery glow; Council Hall (3 members + chairman) with the opinion → ranking → verdict sequence and dissent lantern; Debate Stage, Round Table, Feynman Porch, Panic Line booth, Oral Exam Hut, Rubric Courthouse, Exam Oracle, Devil's TA as landmarks; the mesh view (locked doors, campfire commons, envelopes, red '!' markers, door pulse ring, notice board); the trip cutscene on trip.confirmed; the pure event-to-command adapter covering every VillageEvent in core; LiveSource and ReplaySource exported from the Phaser-free @squad/village/sources; mountVillage({source,onOverlay}) returning {destroy,setCamera}; overlay bridge events; a text-alternative agent list feed; mobile layout; Calm mode; pause when hidden. Use the motion workstream's primitives (docs/motion-api.md, they publish it within 15 minutes; stub until then) and the art from docs/design/asset-manifest.md.

**UI bar:** every frame should look composed: depth from layered parallax, warm window light against cool night, readable bubbles, no sprite jitter (integer positions), no clipped edges, consistent shadows, rhythm in the skyline. Capture canvas frames at several timestamps with Playwright, view them, and compare against the reference collage like an art director; iterate until a stranger would believe it came from a game studio. Verify 60 fps and a replay of every event type.

**Sub-agent ideas:** world and parallax, character and input, buildings and interiors, council hall, mesh view, event adapter and sources, cutscene, a11y list and mobile, scene reviewer.
